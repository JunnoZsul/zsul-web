How we calculate

The base price adjusts by volume and complexity.

Records

The base scope includes up to 50,000 records. Each additional 50,000-record block may add from $50 USD.

Data sources

The base dashboard includes 1 source. Each additional source may add from $85 USD depending on the format or connector.

Views and KPIs

An extra dashboard view can add from $140 USD, depending on filters, metrics and visual complexity.

Automation

APIs, special connections or frequent refreshes are quoted based on the system and update frequency.

Revisions and urgency

Extra revision rounds or rush delivery can adjust the final project price.

How to choose

If you do not know which plan you need, we start with the diagnostic.

You need cleaning if...

You have duplicate databases, incomplete data or files that do not match across teams.

You need a dashboard if...

You already have data, but spend too much time preparing reports or explaining numbers every week.

You need maintenance if...

Your business changes often and your reports need adjustments, new metrics or ongoing support.
