      How we calculate

      The investment depends on scope and complexity.

      Records

      Volume and structure determine the cleaning effort. The entry scope covers up to 50,000 rows and 20 columns combined.

      Data sources

      One or two compatible Excel or CSV sources allow an initial estimate. Three or more sources, systems or special connections require review.

      Views and KPIs

      We define metrics and views with you. Multiple areas, different permissions or more metrics require a specific proposal.

      Automation

      APIs, special connections or real-time information are reviewed according to the system and update frequency.

      Revisions and urgency

      Revision rounds and the schedule are agreed before starting. Scope changes or urgency require reviewing the proposal.

      How to choose

      If you do not know which plan you need, we start with the diagnostic.

      You need cleaning if...

      You have duplicate databases, incomplete data or files that do not match across teams.

      You need a dashboard if...

      You already have data, but spend too much time preparing reports or explaining numbers every week.

      You need maintenance if...

      Your business changes often and your reports need adjustments, new metrics or ongoing support.
